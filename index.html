About

Support is about trust, and trust is earned one ticket at a time.

I am a Senior Technical Support Engineer and team lead based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, working with SaaS and Shopify products for merchants across the US, UK, and EU. My days run on live chat, email, and video, onboarding customers, running needs and technical discovery, and turning confused users into confident, loyal ones.

Beyond solving tickets, I lead a small support team, deliver paid custom functionality for merchants, and channel customer feedback back to the product team. I lean on AI as a daily co-pilot to move faster, and I care most about one thing: every customer leaving better off than they arrived.

Outside work you will find me on a motorcycle, travelling somewhere new, or on a cricket pitch.
