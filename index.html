AI workflow

        
AI drafts. I decide. Every day.

        

          This isn't a buzzword section. It's how my working day actually runs — AI does the first pass,
          I review everything before a customer or a report sees it.
        


        

          

            

              01
              
Vibe coding with Claude Code

              
I build custom store designs, demo stores and new-feature demos — the merchant sees it working before anyone commits to building it.

            

            

              02
              
Trained AI threads for replies

              
Claude and ChatGPT threads loaded with our product knowledge. Paste the customer's message, get an accurate draft, review, send. Google's built-in email AI for quick drafts.

            

            

              03
              
AI-assisted issue reproduction

              
AI inspects the merchant's store with me, so I reproduce reported issues faster and find practical fixes instead of guessing.

            

            

              04
              
MCP + automated QA

              
ClickUp, Google Drive, Figma and a test store connected over MCP; a Playwright + Chromium setup runs most test cases. I review, add manual and edge-case checks, and submit the report.
