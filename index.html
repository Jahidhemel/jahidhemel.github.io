About

Support is about trust, and trust is built one ticket at a time.

I am a Technical Support Engineer and team lead based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Most of my days are spent on live chat, email, and video calls with customers across the US, UK, and EU, figuring out what went wrong and getting it fixed together with the dev team.

I care a lot about clear communication and patient problem solving. My goal in every conversation is simple: the customer should walk away with their problem solved and feeling looked after. I am comfortable with US aligned night shifts and I do well in fast, high volume environments.

When I am not working, you will usually find me on my motorcycle, travelling somewhere new, or on a cricket pitch with friends.
