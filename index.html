About

          
Every customer should leave better off than they arrived.

          

            I have spent a bit over three years in support for Shopify apps, mostly with merchants in the US, UK and
            EU. The job is simple to describe and hard to do well: help people get what they came for, and make sure
            they still like us afterwards.
          

          

            Most of my day is live chat and email, with the odd video call. I ask what a merchant is trying to do, work
            out whether the app can do it, and if it can't, I scope the custom work and we build it for them. I also run
            the support and QA teams, which means rosters, onboarding new people, and signing off releases. AI helps me
            with most of it, but I read everything before it goes out.
          

          

            When I'm not working I'm usually on a motorbike, somewhere new, or on a cricket pitch.

AI workflow

        
How I use AI, every day.

        

          Four things I do with AI most working days. In each case it does the first pass and I check the
          result before a customer or a report sees it.
        


        

          

            

              01
              
Vibe coding with Claude Code

              
I build custom store designs, demo stores and quick demos of new features, so a merchant can see the thing working before anyone commits to building it properly.

            

            

              02
              
Trained AI threads for replies

              
I keep Claude and ChatGPT threads loaded with our product docs. I paste in the customer's message, get a draft back, fix whatever is off, and send it. Gmail's built-in AI covers the quick ones.

            

            

              03
              
AI-assisted issue reproduction

              
I point AI at the merchant's store while I reproduce the issue. It reads the theme and the page faster than I can, which gets me to the real cause sooner.

            

            

              04
              
MCP + automated QA

              
ClickUp, Google Drive, Figma and a test store are connected over MCP, and a Playwright and Chromium setup runs most of our test cases. I go through the results, add the manual and edge-case checks, and send the report.
